Community guidelines

Terms & Conditions

By using Chatify, you agree to help keep conversations safe, lawful, and respectful. These are basic community rules for this early version of the service.

1. Be respectful

Do not bully, harass, threaten, intimidate, or abuse anyone. Slurs, hateful attacks, targeted humiliation, and encouragement of violence are not allowed.

2. Keep sexual content off Chatify

Do not send, request, or share sexually explicit content, sexual solicitation, or unwanted sexual messages. Any sexual content involving a minor is strictly prohibited and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

3. Protect privacy

Do not share another person's private information, intimate images, passwords, or personal messages without their clear permission. Do not impersonate another person.

4. No spam, scams, or malicious activity

Do not flood chats, run scams, send phishing links, distribute malware, or try to access accounts or systems without permission.

5. Share lawful content

Do not use Chatify to plan, promote, or facilitate illegal activity, or to distribute content that violates another person's rights.

6. Use the service responsibly

You are responsible for what you send and for keeping your account credentials secure. Use blocking or reporting tools when available, and contact us about urgent safety concerns.

7. Enforcement and changes

We may remove content or restrict accounts that break these rules, subject to applicable law and available review processes. We may update these terms as Chatify develops; continued use after an update means you accept the updated terms.

These community terms are a starting point for the project and should be reviewed for your local laws before a public production launch.
